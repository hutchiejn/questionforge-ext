# Publishing privacy and contact pages

The extension includes `docs/privacy.html`, `docs/contact.html`, and their local
styles and branding. Keep these files in the distributable ZIP so the in-product
links continue to work without a server.

For external listings and the public website, provide public, working HTTPS
links to the privacy and contact pages. Verify those URLs without signing in;
an extension-local URL is not a public website URL.

Before a release, review the policy against the extension's actual storage,
clipboard, AI-sharing, and optional donation behavior. Update the text when
those practices change. Verify public hosting separately from the local pages.
