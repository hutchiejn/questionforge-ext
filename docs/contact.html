When reporting a problem

Include the QuestionForge version, your browser version, and what you were trying to do. Describe the error, but do not email student records, private exam answer keys, or confidential lesson materials unless absolutely necessary and you have permission.

About the extension

QuestionForge is a free, offline-first Chrome extension for creating and formatting assessments from questions and teacher-reviewed learning materials. It does not require a QuestionForge account or a paid AI API.
