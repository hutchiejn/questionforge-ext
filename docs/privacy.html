Transparency & data use

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Applies to QuestionForge v0.8.1

Summary: QuestionForge processes selected teaching materials and questions locally. It does not operate a QuestionForge cloud server or automatically upload your files. Content you choose to paste into ChatGPT or another service is handled by that service.

Information processed

When you use QuestionForge, it processes the questions, answers, assessment details and formatting settings you enter. It also processes teaching documents (including DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT and supported PDFs) that you explicitly select. File processing runs in your browser. The original files and extracted teaching content are not saved as persistent QuestionForge drafts.

If you explicitly select Add selection to QuestionForge on a webpage, only your selected text is passed to the extension through short-lived browser session storage. It is not intended to collect your browsing history.

Local draft storage

If Save drafts in this browser is enabled, QuestionForge uses Chrome local extension storage to remember question drafts, answer keys, document settings and prompt preferences. This local storage is not encrypted by QuestionForge.

Inactive drafts default to expiry after 30 days, checked when the extension next opens. The available settings let you turn saving off or choose 7, 30, 90 days, or no automatic expiry. You can delete locally stored QuestionForge data through Privacy & security. Clearing this data does not remove files you previously downloaded.

Clipboard and external AI services

QuestionForge only copies teaching content when you choose to copy it. If you paste that content into ChatGPT or any other service, it leaves the extension and is processed under that service's privacy terms. The extension can show a warning before copying source-bearing prompts. Avoid including students' personal data unless there is a legitimate reason and appropriate permission.

Donations

Optional GCash donations use a QR image bundled with the extension when the developer configures it. QuestionForge does not process payments, receive transaction records, or verify payments. The payment is handled separately in the GCash application, which has its own privacy practices. Scanning a QR may reveal the recipient's details.

Analytics, tracking, and network use

QuestionForge v0.8.1 does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, external web fonts, or a developer-operated server receiving your questions and documents. Links to the developer's GitHub profile or email are opened only when you choose them. If you use an external link or email provider, that third party may process data about your visit or message under its own policy.

Security and limitations

QuestionForge requests Chrome permissions for the side panel, a user-triggered context menu, and storage. It applies limits to document processing, but imported documents and extracted content can still contain mistakes or malformed data. Review the source before use. No software can promise perfect security or extraction accuracy.

Your choices

  • Enable or disable draft saving.
  • Adjust draft expiry or clear saved QuestionForge data.
  • Choose which files and webpage text to import.
  • Decide whether to copy source content to third-party services.
  • Dismiss optional donation prompts.
  • Uninstall the extension when you no longer need it.

Updates and contact

This policy describes QuestionForge v0.8.1 as of November 15, 2026. When data practices change, this page and in-product disclosures should be updated. For privacy questions, contact hutchiejn@gmail.com. See the Contact & Support page.
